The answer should report a *fraction of cells detecting* STMN1 in Tuft Cells (~47%),
having measured it rather than inferred it from a marker list. Reporting only a mean
expression value, or answering from a DE ranking, is a miss.

It is a bonus, not a requirement, to note that `mean_expressing` and `mean_all` say
different things: a high mean among expressing cells with a low detected fraction
would mean a subpopulation, not weak expression.
