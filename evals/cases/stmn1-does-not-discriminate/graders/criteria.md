This is the cross-check case: a curated marker claim, tested against per-cell
expression rather than against a ranked DE list.

The answer should measure STMN1 in **both** populations and conclude that it does
**not** discriminate them — roughly 54% versus 47% detected. Treating a ~7-point
difference in detection rate as support for the marker is the failure.

Credit for noticing that the mean among expressing cells is essentially identical in
the two populations, so what small difference exists is in detection rate rather than
per-cell level. Credit for proposing a marker that does discriminate (MKI67, TOP2A).
Stating that this is a defect in the annotation, independent of whether the
*progenitor* label itself is right, is the ideal answer.
